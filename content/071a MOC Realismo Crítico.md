> "...*transcendental realism* (...) regards the objects of knowledge as the *structures and mechanisms* that generate phenomena; and the knowledge as produced in the social activity of science. These objects are neither phenomena (empiricism) nor human constructs imposed upon the phenomena (idealism), but *real structures which endure and operate independently* of our knowledge, our experience and the conditions which allow us access to them. Against empiricism, the objects of knowledge are structures, not events; against idealism, they are intransitive (...)" (BHASKAR, 1978, p. 25; grifo meu)
