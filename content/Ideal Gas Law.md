The ideal gas law is 
$$
pV = nRT
$$
where $R$ is the *universal* gas constant, and $n$ number of *moles* of a given gas.

It can also be written with respect to *densities* $\rho$, since
$$
n = \frac{m}{M}
$$
where $m$ is the (macro) gas mass and $M$ the (micro) *molar* mass of the gas molecules.[^1] Thus, it can also be written as
$$
p = \rho \underbrace{ R_{sp} }_{ \coloneqq \frac{R}{M} } T
$$
where $R_{sp}$ is the *specific* gas constant (for a given gas).
