 **Comment**: Empirical, actual and real; papel de experimentos como para isolar leis DE TENDÊNCIA
> The domain of the real consists ofall the ‘‘mechanisms’’ that exist in the world,which is to say, of all the various levels andtypes of entities with their various powersand tendencies. The domain of the actualconsists of all mechanisms that have beenactivated, even if they have not beenobserved. The domain of the empirical,finally, consists of all mechanisms that havebeen activated and observed. Note that thethree domains are generally ‘‘out of phase’’in everyday experience. The real purposeof a scientific experiment is to bring them‘‘into phase,’’ and so to activate, isolate,and observe the powers and tendencies ofa particular entity or strata. Thus, experi-ments really do reveal laws. But these lawsgovern entities, not events, they describetendencies, not regularities.
